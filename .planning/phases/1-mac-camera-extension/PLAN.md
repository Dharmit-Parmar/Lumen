# Phase 1: Mac Camera Extension - Execution Plan

## 1. Goal
Verify live Android phone frames through the Mac camera extension.

## 2. Steps
1. Move the built host app to `/Applications` and run it.
2. Click install extension from the host app UI.
3. Approve the extension in System Settings -> Privacy and Security.
4. Open Photo Booth and verify the virtual camera appears in the Camera menu.
5. Start the Android USB stream and verify the live phone image appears in Photo Booth and QuickTime.

## 3. Success Criteria
- Live phone frames are visible in Mac camera consumer apps (Photo Booth, QuickTime).
